# Setting up your Wallet

PulsWeb2 is built on top of the Polkadot ecosystem. To use the dApp, you may need a compatible wallet to manage your account and sign actions.

### Step 1: Choose a Wallet
Common wallet options in the Polkadot ecosystem include:
1.  **Polkadot{.js}** (Browser Extension)
2.  **Talisman** (Browser Extension)
3.  **SubWallet** (Browser/Mobile)

### Step 2: Install and Create
Using **Polkadot{.js}** as an example:
1.  Go to the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore) and search for the extension.
2.  Click "Create New Account."
3.  **CRITICAL**: Write down and safely store your **12-word recovery phrase (Seed Phrase)**. Do not take a screenshot or store it in the cloud. This is the only way to recover your assets.
4.  Set a strong wallet password.

### Step 3: Connect to PulsWeb2
1.  Open the PulsWeb2 dApp.
2.  Click **"Connect Wallet"** in the top right corner.
3.  Allow the dApp to access your account in the popup window.
4.  Once connected, you will see your address and account information.

---

**Note**: PulsWeb2 uses the Polkadot address format. Address display may change across networks while referring to the same underlying key material, depending on wallet behavior.
